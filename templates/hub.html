Change the library key

Every member’s devices hold the library’s key, which opens every book link and book file of this library. A removed member’s devices forget it at their next connection; if someone may have kept a copy (a lost phone, an invite in the wrong hands), give the library a new key. Books sent from then on use it; books sent before still open for the members, and for whoever kept the old key.

Delete the library on the hub

Syncs every account a last time, then deletes the library on the hub with every member’s account, their devices and the books there. Marks not yet synced on their devices are lost, and everyone must be invited again. Your marks and positions here stay.
